# Ricochet Zero — Core Design & Gameplay Rules

Always uphold these two foundational fun pillars in every design, balance, rendering, audio, and gameplay change for **Ricochet Zero**:

## 1. Matrix-Style Slow-Mo Bullet Dodging
- A core thrill of the game is entering **0.2x slow-mo (Focus Ring)** mid-chaos to read incoming enemy fire and weave cleanly through multiple projectiles.
- **Readability first:** Enemy projectiles and red-state hazards must remain visually unmistakable during both normal speed and slow-mo (high-contrast crimson silhouettes, clear sniper/boss telegraphs, unobstructed arena sightlines).
- **Preserve the rush:** Never undermine bullet-dodging tension with passive auto-dodge mechanics, free invulnerability crutches, or excessive bullet-erasing defaults—clutch last-second evasion under time dilation should feel earned.

## 2. Meticulous Aiming & Skill Mastery
- Satisfaction comes from **precise, deliberate aiming**—lining up multi-wall ricochet banks, managing Charge Tiers, timing mid-flight **Brake & Momentum Aim** taps along the nose chevron, and threading shots into exposed weak spots (like banking around the Boss's frontal shield).
- **No aim crutches:** Keep aiming deterministic and manual. Do not add auto-aim, bullet homing, or overpowered upgrades that erase the need for careful positioning, angle calculation, and timing.
- **Reward mastery:** Ensure weapons, upgrades, enemy patterns, and physics stay tuned so skillful bank shots and accurate timing feel consistently decisive and rewarding.
